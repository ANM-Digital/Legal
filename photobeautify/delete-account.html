Delete Your PhotoBeautify Account

PhotoBeautify

Developer: ANM Digital Labs

Last Updated: August 16, 2026

You can request deletion of your PhotoBeautify account and the associated app data using the method below. You do not need to reinstall the app to submit a deletion request.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email from the email address associated with your PhotoBeautify account.
  2. Use the subject line PhotoBeautify Account Deletion Request.
  3. State that you want your PhotoBeautify account and associated app data deleted.
  4. We may contact you only if reasonably necessary to verify that the request relates to your account.

Request Account Deletion

Deletion requests can also be sent directly to mutlu@anmconsultancy.com.

What Will Be Deleted

When a verified account-deletion request is completed, we will delete the PhotoBeautify account and the app data associated with that account that is under our control, where applicable. This may include:

  • PhotoBeautify authentication/account records;
  • app entitlement or subscription-status records maintained by PhotoBeautify;
  • app usage or account-preference records associated with the account, if any.
Your photos: PhotoBeautify's current photo-enhancement functions process selected photos locally on your device. Photos selected for editing are not uploaded to PhotoBeautify servers as part of normal photo processing.

Google Play Purchases and Subscriptions

Deleting your PhotoBeautify account does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription or delete transaction records controlled by Google. If you have an active Google Play subscription, manage or cancel it separately through Google Play.

Data That May Be Retained

Limited information may be retained only when reasonably necessary for legal, regulatory, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or accounting obligations. Any retained information will be limited to the purpose that requires its retention and will not be used to keep the deleted PhotoBeautify account active.

Processing of Your Request

We will process verified deletion requests within a reasonably prompt period. If additional information is required to identify the correct account, we will contact you using the email address from which the request was submitted.
