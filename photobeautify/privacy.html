Privacy Policy

PhotoBeautify

Last Updated: August 15, 2026

This mobile application (“Application”) is developed and maintained by ANM Digital Labs, part of ANM Consultancy.

PhotoBeautify is designed with privacy in mind. The Application processes selected photos locally on the user's device during normal photo beautification and does not upload those photos to ANM Consultancy servers or third-party photo-processing services.

Photos and Image Processing

When you choose a photo, the photo remains on your device. PhotoBeautify uses the selected photo only to perform the requested local beautification and enhancement functions.

  • Selected photos are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Selected photos are not sent to third-party photo-processing services.
  • Selected photos are not used for advertising or model training.
  • Beautified results are saved to the device only when you choose to save them.

Personal Information

PhotoBeautify does not require you to provide a name, email address, location, contacts, phone records, or other personal information for normal photo processing.

Local Device Access

The Application may request access needed to let you select photos and save processed results on your device. This access is used only to provide those functions.

Subscriptions and Google Play

If you choose to purchase a subscription through Google Play, payment processing and subscription management are handled by Google Play under Google's own terms and privacy practices. PhotoBeautify does not receive your full payment-card details.

Third-Party Platform Services

The Application may rely on operating-system and distribution-platform services required for normal Android and Google Play functionality. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Application or applicable requirements change. Any update will be posted on this page with a revised date.
