Delete Your Data

PhotoBeautify

This page lets you request deletion of personal data associated with your PhotoBeautify account while keeping your account active.

Photos and Edited Images

PhotoBeautify processes photos locally on your device during normal photo editing. Selected photos and edited results are not uploaded to ANM Digital Labs servers for photo processing. If you want to remove photos or edited images, delete them directly from your device or gallery.

Data You Can Request to Delete

If PhotoBeautify stores account-related information needed for authentication, subscription status, entitlement verification, support, or account operation, you may request deletion of eligible stored data without deleting your whole account.

  • Account-related profile data, where stored and eligible for deletion
  • Support-related information associated with your account, where applicable
  • Other eligible account-linked data that is not required to be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or transaction-record obligations

How to Request Data Deletion

Send a request from the email address associated with your PhotoBeautify account. Please include the subject PhotoBeautify Data Deletion Request and identify the data you want deleted.

Request Data Deletion

What Happens Next

We will review the request, verify the account where necessary, and delete eligible data associated with the request. Some information may need to be retained when required for legal, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, or transaction-record purposes.

Google Play Purchases

Google Play payment and subscription records are handled by Google. Deleting PhotoBeautify account-related data does not automatically delete Google Play transaction history or cancel an active Google Play subscription.

Delete Your Entire Account

If you want to delete your entire PhotoBeautify account and associated eligible data instead, use the separate account deletion page.
